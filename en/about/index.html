About us.

CatSensor started with a simple idea: make the changes hidden in a cat's daily routine easier to see. Our team brings together software, IoT, and a strong focus on animal wellbeing to help owners understand their cats without disrupting their habits.

How we work

We are building CatSensor to make early health signals simple, visible, and actionable at home.

Our work brings together development, field validation, and conversations with cat owners. Every step is designed to create a discreet, understandable tool that is genuinely useful in everyday life.

Key milestones that shaped CatSensor's early journey in 2026.

Partnership with the Pierre-De Saurel animal shelter

The Service animalier Pierre-De Saurel et régions (SAPDSR) agreed to collaborate with CatSensor through an exchange of data about cats.

  • The collaboration will help CatSensor better understand the realities faced by shelters and continue validating its solution using concrete data.
  • The goal is to accelerate the development of feline-health monitoring tools built on reliable, longitudinal data.
  • The partnership was made possible with support from the HUB at Cégep de Sorel-Tracy and Jean-Philippe Hébert.

Hardware development

The summer was focused on building the device and hardware stack.

Visibility and fundraising at the golf tournament

CatSensor was highlighted during the Beauchemin, Fleury, Beauvillier and Aube-Kube golf classic with support from the Fondation du Cegep de Sorel-Tracy and its partners.

  • Genevieve Menard from Descartes Systems Group helped bring attention to the project during the event.
  • The Foundation's gratitude space made it possible to send interns William Beaudin and Samuel Courchesne.
  • Funds raised help support the HUB and innovation projects such as CatSensor, winner of the Parcours Startup Validation block.

1st place - Startup Phase 2 Validation

William and Samuel won first place at Projet Startup Phase 2 - Validation with CatSensor.

  • The project received official recognition for validation quality and potential.
  • The roadmap accelerated toward hardware execution.

May 14, 2026

Partnership and field validation

William and Samuel teamed up during their Systems Development course and identified the problem that would become CatSensor.

  • Interviews were conducted with veterinarians, cat owners, and professionals in bio-impedance.
  • A quantitative validation survey was run with around 100 respondents.
  • The results confirmed the need and helped define product priorities.
